# MW: Revised Air Support

A mod for `Most Wanted` that brings back the police helicopter's `unused radio speech` and fixes the police radio.

## Restore

- `Unused ram speech` for the helicopter. The pilot now switches between his normal and intense lines.
- `Unused search pattern speech.` When the helicopter loses you or the chase goes into cooldown, the pilot calls a search pattern.
- `Unused "spotted you again" speech`, mixed in with the vanilla lines.
- A new helicopter cuts in over the other cops when it joins the chase.
- `Unused "jump right back on" and "hold on station"` lines when a police tactic falls through.
- `Weather reports.` When it rains, the helicopter interrupts about the weather.
- The helicopter requesting a `10-67` and spike strips from dispatch, with dispatch answering.
- `Cops requesting air support` from dispatch after the helicopter is shot down, runs out of fuel or leaves, with dispatch answering the unit by name.
- The helicopter asking dispatch for the suspect's record when it checks in. At heat 5+, dispatch reads out the record.
- The helicopter answering backup calls. If it's up, the pilot reports the cover units he sees closing in. If it isn't up yet or was wrecked, he answers on his way in ("We can take that one, we're only minutes away").
- On the 10 minute mark when the pursuit music shuffles, dispatch uses lines like "Attention patrol members", and when she's done, the helicopter calls out the player's LIVE vehicle, like "Suspect vehicle is a silver Porsche."
- The `helicopter's damage speech` when you take it out, like with a pursuit breaker.
- The `helicopter's reckless driving lines`, as his answer when dispatch asks him for a situation update.

## Fixes and Improvements

- Fixed kill shotting the helicopter making it randomly call "low fuel" and mention "tunnels" instead of its proper damage speech.
- Fixed the pilot saying "You got cover units, go three to your position…" after a backup reminder even with the helicopter destroyed. The game had him say it half of the time, only checking that a pilot radio existed, and that radio stays around after the helicopter goes down.
- Fixed the MW Speech System going quiet in long pursuits.

## Requirements

- Need for Speed: Most Wanted (2005), PC version 1.3.
- Of course, a Ultimate ASI Loader.

## Installation

1. Pretty simple. Just copy over the `scripts` folder in your game directory, and launch the game.

To uninstall, go to `scripts` and delete `NFSMWRevisedAirSupport.asi`.
